Sadzan Rahin (“we,” “our,” or “us”) publishes Mecha-Paws: Turbo Scrap (the “Game”). This policy explains how information is handled when you play the Game, use online features, save your garage to the cloud, or contact us.

This policy describes the current EOS guest version of the Game. The Game supports offline play, local-network matches, internet matches through Epic Online Services (“EOS”), and optional cloud garage saves. You do not need to provide your name, email address, telephone number, or payment details to play. Online features use an installation-linked guest identity rather than a public Epic account sign-in.

## 1. Information stored on your device

The Game stores information locally to provide its features, including:

- Your selected toy bots, regular starter weapons, and matching leg assemblies.
- Garage choices and game preferences, such as sound, screen effects, and touch-control settings.
- Guest authentication information used to reconnect to EOS, when you connect online.
- Temporary information needed to run matches and restore the interface.

Offline play does not require you to connect to EOS. We do not operate an Oracle server, Nakama server, or a separate publisher-hosted gameplay database for this version. This does not mean that online information stays only on your device: EOS processes information for the online features described below.

Your operating system or backup provider may back up app data depending on your device settings. Such backups are managed by that provider.

## 2. Online guest identity and multiplayer

When you choose to connect online, the Game uses EOS to create or access a guest identity associated with the installation. EOS assigns a Product User ID that the Game uses for authentication, room membership, multiplayer connections, and cloud storage.

Online play involves processing:

- Your EOS guest identifier and authentication information.
- Network and connection information, including IP addresses and connection status.
- Room identifiers, room membership, readiness, and connection messages.
- Your selected bot and loadout, movement, attacks, equipment changes, round state, and results needed to synchronize the match.
- Technical information needed to operate, diagnose, and protect the online service.

The Game uses peer-to-peer matches, with one player's device hosting the match. Connections may use Epic relay services. A direct connection can expose network information, including your IP address, to the other peer. Your opponent receives the bot, loadout, and combat information needed to play the match. A room code helps players find a room; it is not a private password.

Local-network matches exchange connection and gameplay information between devices on the same network. The current Game does not provide public player profiles, text chat, voice chat, or a public Epic friends-list interface.

## 3. Optional cloud garage saves

Cloud garage storage is provided through EOS Player Data Storage. When you choose SAVE GARAGE TO CLOUD, the Game uploads your two regular garage builds and a save timestamp, associated with your EOS guest identity. RESTORE CLOUD GARAGE retrieves that information.

The Game does not upload a garage save merely because you edit your local build. Saving and restoring are player-initiated actions.

Guest access is linked to the installation. Clearing app data, uninstalling, reinstalling, or changing devices may prevent you from accessing the same guest identity and its cloud save. There is no public Epic account sign-in or guaranteed cross-device recovery in this version. Removing the app does not, by itself, request deletion of information stored by EOS.

## 4. How information is used

Information is used to:

- Run the Game and remember your local settings and garage choices.
- Authenticate online guests and create, join, and synchronize matches.
- Store and retrieve the cloud garage builds you choose to save.
- Maintain service reliability, diagnose problems, and protect against misuse.
- Respond to support messages and privacy requests.
- Meet applicable legal obligations.

This version does not include an advertising SDK, in-app purchases, or a separate publisher analytics or crash-reporting SDK. We do not sell your personal information or use your garage choices for advertising profiles.

## 5. Third-party services and sharing

The Game integrates Epic Online Services for online authentication, lobbies, peer-to-peer networking, relay connections, and player data storage. Epic processes information under the applicable EOS arrangements and its privacy notices. See [Epic Games' Privacy Policy](https://legal.epicgames.com/epicgames/privacy-policy?lang=en-US) and the [EOS Data Privacy, Security & Trust Commitment](https://onlineservices.epicgames.com/services/terms/trust-statement).

If you download the Game through Google Play, Google separately processes store, installation, and platform information under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Distribution through Google Play does not mean that this Game integrates AdMob or a Google Play Games sign-in SDK.

Our email provider processes messages when you contact support. We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, address fraud or security incidents, or protect the rights and safety of players and others.

## 6. Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include, such as a problem description, device model, online guest ID, or screenshot. We use it to respond, troubleshoot, and handle your request.

Please do not send passwords, authentication tokens, payment information, government identification, or other sensitive information. Remove information unrelated to the issue from screenshots or attachments.

## 7. Storage and retention

- Local data remains on your device until it is changed, cleared, or removed, subject to any operating-system backups.
- Your uploaded cloud garage build remains available under its guest identity until it is replaced or deleted, subject to service availability and Epic's applicable storage arrangements.
- Match state is used to operate the session. This version does not maintain a publisher-operated match-history or leaderboard database. Epic may retain service, diagnostic, or security records under its own applicable policies and arrangements.
- Support correspondence is kept for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the inquiry, related follow-up, security, and legal obligations. You may request deletion of correspondence under our control.

We do not promise a fixed deletion period for records controlled by third-party providers. Some information may need to be retained where required or permitted by applicable law.

## 8. Your choices and data-deletion requests

You can play offline without connecting to EOS. You can leave an online room and disconnect through the Game's online interface.

To delete your uploaded garage build, open SETTINGS, connect to CLOUD GARAGE if needed, choose DELETE CLOUD GARAGE, and confirm. This removes the uploaded garage file associated with the connected guest identity. It keeps your local garage build and does **not** delete your EOS Product User ID or all provider-held records.

To remove local app data, use your Android device's app-storage controls or uninstall the Game. Manage any device backups separately. If you want to remove a cloud save, delete it before clearing local data, while you still have access to the associated guest identity.

For requests to access, correct, or delete information under our control, including support messages or information associated with your online guest identity, email [sadzanrahin1998@gmail.com](mailto:sadzanrahin1998@gmail.com?subject=Mecha-Paws%20Privacy%20or%20Data%20Deletion%20Request) with the subject “Mecha-Paws Privacy or Data Deletion Request.” State what you want removed and include your online guest ID if available. You can copy it from SETTINGS → PRIVACY & SUPPORT → COPY ONLINE ID FOR SUPPORT after connecting online.

We may ask for reasonable information to verify your relationship to the requested data. We will review the request, explain available actions and any limitations, and work with the relevant provider where needed. Deleting the Game's data does not delete an independently held Epic or Google account. For information controlled independently by Epic or Google, you can also use the privacy-request channels in their policies.

Depending on your location, applicable law may give you additional rights to access, correct, erase, obtain a copy of, or restrict or object to the processing of your information, or to complain to a relevant supervisory authority. Contact us to exercise rights relating to information under our control.

Epic, Google, and our email provider may process information in countries other than Bangladesh or your country of residence. Their applicable policies and arrangements describe their international-processing practices and safeguards.

Parents and guardians should review the Game's online features and supervise children's use as appropriate.
